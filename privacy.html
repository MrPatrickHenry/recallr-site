Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Recallr (“Recallr,” “the app,” “we,” “us”) is an iOS app published by Patrick Henry. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Recallr does its work entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.

What Recallr accesses

  • Your photo library (screenshots). With your permission, Recallr reads screenshots from the Photos library on your iPhone so it can help you rediscover them. It requests read access only and never modifies or deletes anything in your Photos library.

What Recallr does with it

  • On-device text recognition. Recallr uses Apple’s built-in Vision framework to read text from your screenshots. This happens locally on your device.
  • On-device organization. Recallr assigns each screenshot a category, title, and short summary using logic that runs locally on your device.
  • Local storage only. The derived information (extracted text, category, and a small thumbnail) is stored in a private database on your device using Apple’s SwiftData. It is used only to display your in-app feed.

What Recallr does not do

  • We do not collect or transmit your screenshots, their contents, or any derived data off your device.
  • There is no account, login, or cloud sync. Recallr has no backend server.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Data retention and deletion

All Recallr data lives on your device. You can clear Recallr’s local index at any time from Settings → Delete local index inside the app; this removes Recallr’s database only and leaves your Photos library untouched. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

Children’s privacy

Recallr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. Because Recallr collects no data at all, no personal information about any user is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Recallr handles information, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
